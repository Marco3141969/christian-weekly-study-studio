How to Install on Your Phone

Why does it sometimes say "Unavailable"?

Some preview environments or browsers restrict direct installation prompts due to security policies or iframe sandboxing. But you can always install it manually in 2 seconds using your browser's main menu!

Android (Chrome / Samsung Internet)

  1. Tap the three dots menu () in the top-right corner of your browser.
  2. Look for "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  3. Tap Add or Install. It will appear right on your home screen!

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share button () at the bottom or top of Safari.
  2. Scroll down the menu and tap "Add to Home Screen" ().
  3. Tap Add in the top right.
